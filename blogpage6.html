Way back home

Sorry if I am repeating but, I got home from Portugal, the travel was actually a journey by itself, mom welcomed me with lard and bread and after I slept my deficit off my brain started to think back again. I mean, you start to recognize you are going away from home, when you start feeling, place you called home is becoming something temporary. I was in Slovakia for two weeks, I tried to squeeze out of it as much as possible. Thank god, that the work I wanted to do didn't work out, little bit of climbing, one last ball with boys, drinks and then finish it at cottage acting like I have birthday. In the middle of all this, uncle took me for a trip. Making me a man again, behind the rides of a mountainbike. One day of that, bit of climbing and a lot of cure from sickness. Two months in Portugal I was healthy as I have ever been, then I come home I get instantly sick. Some more cooking, meeting friends and long story short, after 16 hours of commute, bit of bitching and making both of my parents cry I am left in Nijmegen.

The family

My own room, my own time, bike, everything. I was the first one in the house, I was just expecting people, waiting and waiting. Life has beaten me down a few times and from then on, I don't have big expectations from most of the things, kind of self defence mechanism. I was prepared for the worst housemates just to say hi when we meet at the hallway and that is all out of the interaction. I was 3 days deep in living here. I got my bike, and I was outside repairing it. Past me walks a guy with too much bags. For a while I thought, it might be my new roomie. But as he had a helper and walked to the next street, I thought that he will be a neighbour at best. I have no clue why but he came back, either he liked me repairing my bike or the house itself. Stuck the key in the door and introduced him to my dirty oily hands. Evan he is, Evan the Blindfuck chairmaster first. I was finally not living alone, he could finally set himself to new timezone and I went downtown for my first job interview.
No time to spare, because it is still warm and sunny. Me and Evan start our Nijmegen dating in the Vaal. Some good swim, sunsets and everything near to it. This was the gezelich start of the life here, next week we saw eachother barely. It was introweek, god, I have never partied so much, never bedore so many rumors were going around me. I turned into party machine for a week. Wake up socialize yourself party until 4, wake up at 9 and repeat. It was great, but repeating week like this would be quite dangerous. I understood the esence of the dutch culture, pregame as hard as possible, with bus preffered, kiss some dutchies get sober at the party by dancing so next day there is no hangover. It worked, only problem that the sleep deficit added so much up that at the end of the week I fell ill again.
You need to cool of after week like that, graduately. From then parties started with Evan, not too many, not too drunk, but new and new people. I found my work in the nearby store, started dating again and life continued to make sence.
The little moments of biking back home, sun in your face, tired and happy. Life suddenly feels like one of the Mac de Marco's songs. At least it felt like it until...
