Way back home

Sorry if I am repeating but, I got home from Portugal, the travel was actually a journey by itself, mom welcomed me with lard and bread and after I slept my deficit off my brain started to think back again. I mean you start to recognize you are going away from home, when you start feeling, place you call home is becoming something temporary. I was home for two weeks, I tried to squeeze out of it as much as possible. Thank god that the work I wanted to do didn't work out, climbing, one last ball, drinks and then finish it at cottage acting like I have birthday. In the middle of all this, uncle took me for a trip. Making me a man again, once again behind the bar of mountainbike. One day of that, bit of climbing and a lot of cure from sickness. Two months in Portugal I was healthy as I have ever been, then I come home I get instantly sick. Some more cooking, meeting friends and long story short, after 16 hours of commute, bit of bitching and making both of my parents cry I am left in Nijmegen.
